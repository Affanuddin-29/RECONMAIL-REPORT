Research Limits and Presentation Guardrails

Area Current Limitation Required Next Step
Encrypted traffic Passive PCAP analysis generally exposes metadata and handshake information rather than decrypted message content. Focus assessment on observable protocol and cryptographic evidence.
PCAP quality Missing packets can limit session reconstruction. Record partial-analysis states.
Certificate chains Not every capture necessarily contains a complete chain. Distinguish unavailable evidence from invalid certificates.
AI analysis AI output should not establish cryptographic facts. Ground AI explanations in deterministic findings.
Prototype data Current dashboard values may represent demonstration data. Replace with measured results when the forensic backend is implemented.
Presentation Guardrail

RECONMAIL is positioned as a passive forensic assessment framework. The prototype should clearly distinguish demonstrated functionality, synthetic demonstration data, and future backend capabilities.

13

Judge Q&A

What exactly does RECONMAIL analyse?

Captured network traffic containing SMTP, IMAP and POP3 communications, with emphasis on session reconstruction, TLS negotiations, certificates and cryptographic posture.

Does RECONMAIL decrypt email content?

The passive framework focuses on observable network and cryptographic evidence. Encrypted message content is not assumed to be available from an ordinary PCAP.

Why use AI?

AI assists with risk classification, anomaly detection, prioritization and explanation after deterministic technical evidence has been extracted.

What makes the system different from Wireshark?

Wireshark provides extensive packet and protocol visibility. RECONMAIL focuses that evidence into an automated cryptographic security posture assessment and remediation workflow.

How is the security score calculated?

The posture representation is based on assessed dimensions such as TLS configuration, certificates, cryptographic algorithms, protocol configuration and forward secrecy, with the contributing findings exposed to the analyst.

How will the prototype be validated?

Through controlled synthetic email captures representing weak and hardened configurations, followed by comparison of extracted evidence, findings and posture.
